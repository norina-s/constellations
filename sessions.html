Group work

Workshops

Constellations in a group, online or in person. A gentler first step for many, as it is an opportunity to experience the process at lower cost and with the choice whether or not to bring a personal issue. You can attend to stand as a representative, or to witness another's constellation.

Your session

Once you've decided to book a session, we meet online via Zoom, or in person. I guide you through setting up a 'constellation' of the issue, to reveal any hidden dynamics at play.

Integration

The insights gained from just one session can feel deeply profound, even radical, affecting long-held perspectives and unconscious beliefs. Most people take time to allow these changes to settle before deciding on next steps.
