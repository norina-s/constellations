Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who I am

I am Norina Shorter, and I am responsible for the personal information collected through norinashorter.com. You can contact me at norina@norinashorter.com.

What I collect and why

Newsletter. If you subscribe, I collect your email address so I can send you occasional notes and news of workshops and events. I do this with your consent, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.

Discovery calls and sessions. If you book a call, the booking service collects your name, email address and the time you choose. I use this to arrange and hold the call. I may keep brief notes about our conversation so that I can support you. Online sessions are not recorded unless by request. Once the recording is shared with you, the client, it is deleted from my storage.

Event tickets. If you buy a ticket, the ticketing service collects the details needed to process your booking and send you event information.

Client agreement When you book your first paid session, you will be provided with a client agreement which I will ask you to confirm, via email, that you have read in full before the start of our session. I will then store your written confirmation for record keeping purposes (see below).

Who else handles your information

To do the above I use these services, which process information on my behalf: Cal.com and Google Calendar (booking), Ticket Tailor (event tickets), Substack (newsletter), and my email provider. Each has its own privacy policy.

I do not sell your information.

How long I keep your information

Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. Personal contact details provided for sessions or workshops such as phone numbers and email addresses are kept while we are working together and for up to two years after our last session, then deleted. Session notes and your client agreement are kept securely for six years, in line with insurance and professional guidance, then deleted. Payment records are kept for six years for tax purposes.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, or to object to how I use it. Email me and I will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
