Common questions

What actually happens in a session?+

After a short conversation about what you’d like to work on, I guide you in creating a “constellation”, a living map of the people or elements involved. In groups, other participants stand in as representatives. In one to one sessions we use objects or guided visualisation. As the picture unfolds, what’s hidden often becomes clear surprisingly quickly.

How much does it cost?+

In person, a session is £175 for up to 1hr30, or £250 for 2hrs. Online sessions are £125 for up to 1hr30. Longer sessions are available on request. Ongoing coaching is bespoke, and is quoted following an initial consultation. Workshop ticket prices are shown with each event. The 15min discovery call is free, so you can ask any questions before you decide.

Do I need to know anything beforehand?+

Not at all. Most people arrive with curiosity and an issue they want to understand better. I explain everything as we go.

Is this connected to a particular religion?+

No. Constellations work draws on systemic and psychotherapeutic principles rather than any one tradition. Many people find it touches something spiritual, and how you understand that is entirely up to you.

What if it brings up difficult emotions?+

It can. The work often surfaces things that have been unspoken for a long time. Sessions are held with care, at a pace that respects what you can take in, and you’re never asked to go further than feels right.

Is it a replacement for therapy?+

No. Constellations is often described as the shortest of short term therapies: an intervention that can open new insight or movement. For complex or long-standing trauma it works best alongside longer term support.

Is this suitable for everyone?+

This work can bring up deep feeling, and it’s held with care throughout. It isn’t recommended for anyone currently in an acute mental health crisis or experiencing psychosis. If that’s you, I’m happy to help point you towards more suitable support first.
